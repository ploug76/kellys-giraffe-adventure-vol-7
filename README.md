# Kelly's Giraffe Adventure Vol. 7: Party Parade

It's Kelly's birthday! Grumpy Sir Grumpus blew all her animal friends away, so Kelly the giraffe races across the land to gather up all 10 of them and bring them to her party on Party Hill, where Peter is waiting with the cake and balloons. 10 levels in 5 painted worlds (Sunny Meadow, Willow Creek, Frosty Peaks, Firefly Forest, Sunset Boardwalk) and 5 silly bosses. Every friend you find joins your party train and follows you to the party.

Play: https://ploug76.github.io/kellys-giraffe-adventure-vol-7/

Controls: swipe and hold anywhere to move, swipe up or JUMP to jump. ACTION: head bonk, neck ram (double-tap), tongue, ring swing, neck grab (hold UP at a ledge) and neck pound (DOWN+ACTION in the air). Keyboard: arrows/WASD, Z/Space jump, X action, C ram, P pause, M sound. Controllers: stick/D-pad move, A jump, X/B action, Y ram, Start pause.

All characters are original.
